FAQ

Common questions.

What is Sentinel Client?

A mod for Gorilla Tag you control from a disc on your wrist. It tells you who else is running a client, lets you report someone without leaving VR, and adds the things the game never shipped with: nametags, a friends list, lobby hop, colour themes and your own keybinds. New features get added as people ask for them.

Does it affect performance?

No. Sentinel is built to be lightweight. The tablet and checkers run without touching your framerate.

What is lifetime access?

One payment and the tier stays on your account permanently. No renewals, and every future update is included. Premium is $39.99 and Sentinel+ is $59.99 . Patreon has no one-off option, so it is arranged by hand: open a ticket in the Discord and we'll sort it out with you.

Monthly or lifetime, what's the difference?

Both unlock exactly the same features. Monthly is $5.99 for Premium and $9.99 for Sentinel+ through Patreon, cancel any time. Lifetime is a single payment ($39.99 and $59.99) and never renews. The Free tier stays free either way.

How do I cancel?

Everything runs through Patreon, so you cancel there: Patreon → your memberships → Sentinel Client. You keep your access until the period you've already paid for runs out, and the Discord role comes off automatically when it does.

How do I get the download after paying?

Sign in with Discord before you buy, and everything is automatic. The moment your payment goes through, your access is applied and our bot DMs you a receipt. Head to your account and the download is unlocked. Monthly plans renew on their own; you'll get a DM each time.

How do I install it?

Download the installer from your account, run it once, and Sentinel keeps itself updated with every game patch.

Can I get a refund?

No, digital licences aren't refundable, because your access and the download are applied the second you pay and can't be handed back. You can cancel on Patreon whenever you like and you keep everything you've already paid for until it runs out. Billing mistakes are different: a double charge, a charge after cancelling, or access that never arrived all get sorted. The detail is on the Refund Policy page, and the Free tier is there so you can try it properly first.

Where do I get support?

Through our Discord . Sentinel+ members get a priority support channel and an exclusive role.

Refund Policy

What happens if you change your mind, get charged twice, or something goes wrong.

Last updated 3 September 2026

Short version: we don't refund digital licences. The moment your payment goes through, the tier is on your account and the download is unlocked. That can't be handed back, so all sales are final.

What we do instead is cancel cleanly and fix billing mistakes, which is what most people actually need. Both are below.

1 Why it works this way

There's nothing to return. Access is applied instantly, the client is downloadable straight away, and once someone has had the files there's no way to un-give them. Refunding after delivery would mean handing the software out for free to anyone willing to ask, which is exactly how paid mods die.

So the price stays low, the Free tier stays genuinely usable, and you can try Sentinel properly before paying a penny.

2 Cancelling instead

Every subscription can be cancelled at any time, and cancelling is not the same as a refund. Go to Patreon, then your memberships, then Sentinel Client, and cancel there.

You keep full access for the rest of the period you've already paid for (you're not cut off on the day you cancel) and nothing is charged again after that. Your Discord role comes off by itself when the time runs out.

3 When we will always put it right

These are billing errors rather than refunds, and we sort them without arguing:

  • You were charged twice for the same period.
  • You were charged after cancelling , and the cancellation went through before the renewal date.
  • Your access never arrived and we can't get it working, if we can't fix it, we refund it.
  • You were charged for a tier you never got because something on our side failed.

Tell us within 30 days of the charge and include your Discord name and the date. Where the money went through Patreon, some of these have to be resolved on their side, and we'll tell you which.

4 When we won't

  • You changed your mind, or stopped playing Gorilla Tag.
  • You forgot to cancel before the renewal date.
  • You bought it and didn't use it.
  • Your PC can't run it, or you didn't install .NET 8, check the requirements before you buy, and ask us first if you're unsure.
  • A game update temporarily broke a feature and we're fixing it.
  • A feature works differently than you expected. Ask in the Discord before buying and we'll tell you exactly what it does.
  • You were banned from Gorilla Tag, from a lobby, or from our Discord.
  • Your access was revoked for sharing a key, cracking the client, or breaking the Terms of Service .

5 Bought the wrong tier

We'd rather fix that than refund it. Open a ticket and we'll move you to the right tier and settle the difference, upgrading costs the difference, and if you went too high we'll credit the extra as time on your account. Do it before you've used the tier you bought.

Opening a chargeback or a payment dispute without contacting us revokes your access immediately and permanently, including any lifetime purchase, and bans the account from buying again.

Some countries give you a statutory right to cancel an online purchase within a set number of days. Nothing on this page takes away a right you have by law that can't be signed away, and if the law where you live says otherwise, the law wins. If you think that's your situation, open a ticket and tell us where you are.
